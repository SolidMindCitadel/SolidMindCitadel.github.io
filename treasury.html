    Backup and restore

    Why backups matter: all heroes, coins, ranks, tests, trophies and settings live only in this browser on this device. They are not stored online. If this browser's data is cleared, the device is lost, or you switch devices, everything is gone, unless you have a backup.

    A backup also holds the test coin amounts and the Treasury PIN (scrambled). Make one now and then, and keep it somewhere safe, like your own email or files. Never upload a backup to the GitHub repository: it is public, and backups contain the kids' nicknames.

    Restore from a backup

    Warning: restoring replaces everything on this device with the backup: every hero, coin, rank, test, trophy and setting, the test coin amounts and the PIN. Anything here that isn't in the backup will be lost.
